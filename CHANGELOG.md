# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (beta)

- The first public version.
- Three editions: Minimap, Minimap + Fishing and Full.
- One-click installers. The installer of a different edition replaces the installed edition and keeps your settings.
- The uninstaller asks if it must also delete your settings.
- When the game updates, Toshalia tells you to look for a new version on the Discord server.
